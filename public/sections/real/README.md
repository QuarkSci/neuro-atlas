# Real slices

Oblique slices of the BigBrain volume (MNI ICBM152 2009b symmetric space, 100 µm), one per cross-section
level, cut by `pipeline/tools/real_slices.py` on the plane and window listed in `pipeline/section_frames.json`.

Source: Amunts K et al. BigBrain: An ultrahigh-resolution 3D human brain model. Science 2013; 340(6139):1472–1475.
doi:10.1126/science.1235381 — https://bigbrainproject.org
