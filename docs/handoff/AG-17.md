Branch: `ag/AG-17` (asos: `origin/sx/main` `e07dede`) · Sana: 2026-11-24

## Nima qilindi

- `src/or/drill/dust.ts`:
  - `LAYER_FEEL` (`Record<BoneLayer, LayerFeel>`): barcha 7 qatlam (`air`, `scalp`, `outer-table`, `diploe`, `inner-table`, `dura`, `brain`) uchun chang rangi, miqdori, akustik pitch koeffitsiyenti va yuklanish (`load`) jadvali (masalan, `diploe` da pitch pasayadi va chang qorayadi, `inner-table` da keskin yuqori ton).
  - `LAYER_NAMES` (`LayerNames`): barcha 7 qatlam uchun `uz` / `en` HUD nomlari.
  - `createDust` (`CreateDust` / `DustEmitter`): oldindan ajratilgan typed array'li (kadrda 0 GC) Three.js `Points` emitteri; MNI mm koordinatalarni `mniToScene` orqali OR sahna fazosiga o'tkazadi; gravitatsiya `-z` o'qida (chunki OR xonasida `+z` yuqoriga); `max` limitini qat'iy saqlaydi, zarrachalarni muddati tugagach o'chiradi, `clear()` va `dispose()` ni to'liq qo'llaydi.
- `src/or/drill/drillAudio.ts`:
  - `drillPitchHz(s: DrillState)`: RPM, qatlam pitchi va qarshilik ostida RPM sag (cho'kish) ni hisoblovchi sof funksiya.
  - `createDrillAudio` (`CreateDrillAudio` / `DrillAudio`): tashqi audio faylsiz WebAudio sintezatori (`start()` faqat foydalanuvchi bosishida boshlanadi); shpindel g'uvillashi (`sawtooth` + 2× `triangle` garmonika) + suyak qirish shovqini (band-pass filtrli oq shovqin) + `layer`, `plunge-warning` (ikki marta ogohlantirish signali), `breakthrough` (teshib o'tish signali) va `stall` hodisa tovushlari; `mute()` va `dispose()`.
  - `hapticPulseForState`, `hapticPulseForEvent` va `createDrillHaptics` (`CreateDrillHaptics` / `DrillHaptics`): `Gamepad.vibrationActuator.playEffect('dual-rumble', ...)` uchun tezlik chegaralangan (≥ 45 ms) titrash drayveri; geympad yo'qligida xatosiz no-op. (`SURGERY.md` §6 jadvalida faqat `dust.ts` va `drillAudio.ts` ruxsat etilgani uchun alohida `haptics.ts` yaratilmay, `drillAudio.ts` dan eksport qilindi.)
